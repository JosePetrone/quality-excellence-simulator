| # | Sprint | Status | Why it matters |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 001 | Discovery and architecture | done | Define the first pilot and record the decisions needed before implementation. |

| 007 | Full facilitated rehearsal | planned | Measure full-session pacing and verify teams can use quarter feedback before pilot invitations. |

## Notes

Engine implementation depends on an approved rule contract. Inviting teams depends on isolation and recovery checks.
